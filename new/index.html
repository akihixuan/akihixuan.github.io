About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Shenzhen University, in a joint doctoral training program with Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology.

I am advised by Prof. Min Yang, Prof. Shiwen Ni, and Prof. Pengfei Xu.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from Nanyang Technological University and my B.Eng. from Harbin Institute of Technology.

For more information, please see my [CV].

Research Interests

My research interests are large language models (LLMs) and AI for Science. My previous work spans egocentric vision, 3D hand reconstruction, and neuro-symbolic reasoning.

Research & Thesis

  • Egocentric hand action understanding: from multi-view geometric reconstruction to monocular neuro-symbolic reasoning

    Yunxuan Zou

    Master’s thesis, Nanyang Technological University, 2026.

    [Thesis record] [BibTeX]

    This work studies fine-grained hand action understanding from an egocentric perspective. It connects 3D hand geometry with explicit physical states and language-model reasoning. The method is a proof of concept evaluated on a custom wrist-worn dataset.
